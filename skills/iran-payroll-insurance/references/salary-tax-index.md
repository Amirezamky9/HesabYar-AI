# Statutory Index: Iranian Salary Tax Law (مالیات بر درآمد حقوق)

**Primary Statute:** Direct Tax Law of Iran (قانون مالیات‌های مستقیم مصوب ۱۳۶۶ با اصلاحات بعدی، به‌ویژه قانون اصلاحی ۱۳۹۴).  
**Domain Authority:** Iranian National Tax Administration (سازمان امور مالیاتی کشور - INTA).

---

## 1. Scope & Taxable Income (Articles 82 & 83)

### Article 82
- Income derived by a natural person in the service of another party (legal or natural) in return for work performed in Iran, whether in cash or in kind, is subject to Salary Income Tax (مالیات بر درآمد حقوق).

### Article 83
- Taxable salary income consists of the base salary and regular allowances (مزایای مربوط به شغل), whether continuous or discontinuous, before deductions, minus statutory exemptions.

---

## 2. Annual Exemption & Progressive Brackets (Articles 84 & 85)

### Article 84 (معافیت سالانه حقوق)
- The baseline annual salary tax exemption is determined each year in the annual State Budget Law (قانون بودجه کل کشور).
- Monthly calculation:
  $$\text{Monthly Exemption} = \frac{\text{Annual Budget Law Exemption Ceiling}}{12}$$
- Income up to the monthly exemption is taxed at **0%**.

### Article 85 (نرخ و پلکان‌های مالیاتی)
- Tax rates on taxable salary income exceeding the Article 84 exemption are progressive marginal rates established in the annual Budget Law:
  - Tier 1: Income above exemption up to Threshold 1 $\rightarrow$ **10%**
  - Tier 2: Income above Threshold 1 up to Threshold 2 $\rightarrow$ **15%**
  - Tier 3: Income above Threshold 2 up to Threshold 3 $\rightarrow$ **20%**
  - Tier 4: Income above Threshold 3 up to Threshold 4 $\rightarrow$ **25%**
  - Tier 5: Income above Threshold 4 $\rightarrow$ **30%**
- **Marginal Invariant:** Each marginal tax rate applies strictly to the incremental portion of taxable income within that tier, never to the entire income.

## 3. Statutory Exemptions (Article 91 & Court Rulings)

Under Article 91 of the Direct Tax Law and binding administrative precedents:

### 100% Tax-Exempt Compensation Items
1. **Severance Pay & Terminal Gratuity (حق سنوات / مزایای پایان کار):** Article 91, Clause 5. Fully exempt from income tax.
2. **Unused Leave Compensation (بازخرید مرخصی):** Article 91, Clause 5. Fully exempt upon termination or retirement.
3. **Mission Allowance (فوق‌العاده ماموریت):** Article 91, Clause 6. Exempt up to statutory Labor Law limits when supported by official mission orders.
4. **Child Allowance (حق اولاد):** Taxable under general rules, though some historical circulars contested it; under prevailing INTA practice it remains taxable income.
5. **Annual Eidi Bonus (عیدی و پاداش سالانه):** Exempt up to $1/12\text{th}$ of the annual Article 84 exemption ceiling (equivalent to one month's basic tax exemption). Any excess is taxable at the employee's marginal rate.

### 2/7th Health Insurance Deduction (معافیت دو هفتم بیمه سهم کارگر)
- **Legal Precedent:** INTA Circular No. 232/24013/d dated 1384/09/20, reinforced by General Assembly of the Administrative Court decisions.
- **Rule:** Out of the 7% Social Security contribution paid by the employee, $\frac{2}{7}$ represents medical and health treatment insurance under Article 137 of the Direct Tax Law.
- **Calculation:**
  $$\text{Deductible Health Insurance} = \text{Employee 7% Insurance Contribution} \times \frac{2}{7}$$
  $$\text{Taxable Net Base} = \text{Gross Taxable Earnings} - \text{Deductible Health Insurance} - \text{Other Statutory Exemptions}$$

### Welfare Benefits Exemption (مزایای رفاهی و انگیزه‌ای - بن و مسکن)
- **Legal Precedents:**
  - General Assembly of the Administrative Court Ruling No. 1957 dated 1399/12/16.
  - General Assembly Ruling No. 140009970905811257 dated 1400/08/11.
  - INTA Circular No. 200/1401/10 dated 1401/02/10.
- **Rule:** Non-job-specific welfare and motivational allowances—specifically Housing Allowance (حق مسکن), Grocery Allowance (بن کارگری / اقلام مصرفی), and child education/transport assistance—do not constitute remuneration for direct job duties under Article 83 and are **tax-exempt** up to approved statutory decrees.
- **Cautionary Operational Profile:** If a tax office audits a tenant under an older or conservative interpretation, the system supports versioned policy profiles, with the court-precedent profile requiring explicit professional sign-off.

## 4. Employer Withholding & Filing Obligations (Article 86)

### Article 86 (تکلیف کارفرما در کسر و ایصال مالیات حقوق)
- Employers are legally required to calculate, withhold, and remit salary tax for each monthly payroll cycle.
- **Filing & Remittance Deadline:** By the **last day of the following calendar month** (همراه با تسلیم فهرست حقوق‌بگیران به اداره امور مالیاتی محل).
- Electronic Filing: Submission must occur via the official INTA Salary Tax Portal (سامانه مالیات بر درآمد حقوق سازمان امور مالیاتی) in compliant XML / CSV / JSON schema format.
- Penalty for Non-Compliance (Article 197 & 199): Failure to submit the list incurs a 2% fine of the total salary fund; failure to remit withheld tax incurs a 10% penalty on unpaid tax plus 2.5% per month of delay.
