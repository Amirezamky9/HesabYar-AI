# Statutory Index: Iranian Labor Law (قانون کار جمهوری اسلامی ایران)

**Primary Statute:** Labor Law ratified on 1369/08/29 (1990-11-20) by the Expediency Discernment Council.  
**Domain Authority:** Ministry of Cooperatives, Labour, and Social Welfare (وزارت تعاون، کار و رفاه اجتماعی) & Supreme Labor Council (شورای عالی کار).

---

## 1. Wages & Compensation (Articles 34–47)

### Article 34 (حق‌السعی)
- Defines total compensation (*Haqq-as-Sa'i*): All legal receipts of a worker arising from the employment contract, including wage (مزد یا حقوق), housing allowance, grocery voucher, family allowance, transport, in-kind bonuses, and productivity incentives.

### Article 35 & 36 (مزد و مزد ثابت)
- **Wage (مزد):** Cash or non-cash remuneration paid in return for performance of work.
- **Fixed Wage (مزد ثابت):** Aggregate of base wage (مزد شغل / پایه) and fixed job-related allowances (مزایای به تبع شغل) paid for job nature, difficulty, or seniority. Fixed wage forms the baseline for statutory multipliers (overtime, shift, night work, severance).
- General welfare allowances (مزایای رفاهی و انگیزه‌ای مانند بن، مسکن و خواربار) are not part of the fixed wage for statutory multiplier calculations unless explicitly agreed in collective contracts.

### Article 41 (حداقل دستمزد - Minimum Wage)
- The Supreme Labor Council (شورای عالی کار) determines annual minimum wages based on inflation announced by the Central Bank of Iran and household subsistence basket criteria.
- **Invariant:** It is strictly illegal to pay any full-time worker less than the approved minimum daily wage for that calendar year.

### Article 46 (فوق‌العاده ماموریت - Mission Allowance)
- Payable to employees traveling at least 50 km from their principal workplace or required to lodge overnight.
- Statutory floor: Must not be less than **one day's base wage** per day of mission.
- The employer is legally obligated to provide or reimburse round-trip transportation costs.
- **Tax/Insurance Status:** Non-taxable up to statutory limits with verified mission documentation; exempt from Social Security insurance contributions.

## 2. Working Hours, Overtime, Shifts & Rest (Articles 51–63)

### Article 51 (ساعات کار موظف - Working Hours)
- Standard statutory working hours are strictly **44 hours per week**.
- In agricultural and continuous industries, weekly hours may be balanced over consecutive weeks provided average weekly hours do not exceed 44.
- In standard payroll calculation:
  - Daily standard hours: $44 \div 6 = 7.333333$ hours ($7$ hours and $20$ minutes).
  - Nominal monthly divisor: $30 \times 7.333333 = 220$ hours (or contractually established equivalent, e.g., $191.29$ hours).
  - Hourly rate formula: $\text{Hourly Wage} = \text{Daily Base Wage} \div 7.333333$.

### Article 58 (فوق‌العاده شب‌کاری - Night Work Premium)
- Hours worked between **22:00 and 06:00** by non-shift workers incur a mandatory premium of **35%**:
  $$\text{Night Work Rate} = \text{Regular Hourly Rate} \times 1.35$$

### Article 59 (فوق‌العاده اضافه‌کاری - Overtime Pay)
- Overtime requires mutual agreement and is limited to a maximum of 4 hours per day (except emergency or rescue operations).
- Overtime rate multiplier is strictly **140%**:
  $$\text{Overtime Hourly Rate} = \text{Regular Hourly Rate} \times 1.40$$

### Article 56 (فوق‌العاده نوبت‌کاری - Shift Work)
For work rotating throughout the month:
1. Rotating Morning & Afternoon (صبح و عصر): **110%** (+10% premium on base wage).
2. Rotating Morning, Afternoon & Night (صبح، عصر و شب): **115%** (+15% premium on base wage).
3. Rotating Morning & Night OR Afternoon & Night (صبح و شب یا عصر و شب): **122.5%** (+22.5% premium on base wage).

### Article 62 (فوق‌العاده جمعه‌کاری - Friday Work Premium)
- Friday is the mandatory weekly rest day with full pay.
- Workers required to work on Friday are entitled to a **40% premium** (140% total pay for the day), provided an alternative day off during the week is designated as rest.

## 3. Leave & Vacations (Articles 64–74)

### Article 64 (مرخصی استحقاقی سالانه - Entitled Annual Leave)
- Entitled annual paid leave is **26 working days** per calendar year (excluding official public holidays and Fridays).
- In hard or hazardous jobs (مشاغل سخت و زیان‌آور), entitled leave is **36 working days** per year (Art. 65).
- Monthly accrual: Workers accrue $\approx 2.16$ days of paid leave per worked month ($26 \div 12$).

### Article 66 (ذخیره مرخصی - Leave Carryover)
- A worker cannot carry forward more than **9 working days** of unused annual leave into the subsequent calendar year.
- Any remaining unused leave above 9 days must either be taken, compensated, or forfeited pursuant to internal company policies and collective agreements.

### Article 71 (بازخرید مرخصی - Unused Leave Settlement)
- Upon contract termination, retirement, disability, or plant shutdown, the employer must compensate all accrued, unused entitled leave based on the employee's final daily base wage.
- **Tax/Insurance Status:** Exempt from Social Security contributions and exempt from salary tax under Direct Tax Law Art. 91.

### Article 73 (مرخصی اضطراری - Emergency Paid Leave)
- 3 days of fully paid leave upon:
  1. Permanent marriage of the worker.
  2. Death of spouse, father, mother, or children.

### Article 74 (مرخصی استعلاجی - Sick Leave)
- Sick leave verified by the Social Security Organization Medical Commission counts toward the worker's service seniority.
- Wage compensation during illness is paid directly by the Social Security Organization under Social Security Law Articles 59–63 (غرامت دستمزد ایام بیماری), NOT from the employer's operational wage fund.

### Maternity Leave (مرخصی زایمان)
- Pursuant to Article 67 of the Social Security Law and subsequent statutory amendments (Law on Family and Youth Protection 1400), maternity leave is **9 months** (or 12 months for twin births), fully funded and paid by the Social Security Organization.

## 4. Contract Termination & Severance Pay (Articles 21–33)

### Article 24 & 31 (حق سنوات / پاداش پایان کار - Severance Pay)
- Upon termination, expiration of fixed-term contract, retirement, or disability, an employer is legally required to pay severance:
  - Minimum rate: **At least 30 days of the most recent base wage** for each full year of continuous service.
  - Proration applies for partial years of service.
- **Tax/Insurance Status:** Severance pay is **100% exempt from Social Security insurance** and **100% exempt from salary tax** (Direct Tax Law Art. 91, Clause 5).

## 5. Annual Eidi & New Year Bonus (قانون عیدی و پاداش سالانه - مصوب ۱۳۷۰)

- **Formula:** 60 days of base wage for each full year of service:
  $$\text{Gross Eidi} = \text{Daily Base Wage} \times 60$$
- **Ceiling:** The total gross bonus cannot exceed **90 days of the statutory minimum daily wage**:
  $$\text{Eidi Ceiling} = \text{Minimum Daily Wage of the Year} \times 90$$
- **Proration:** Employees with less than 12 months service receive:
  $$\text{Prorated Eidi} = \min\left(\frac{60 \times \text{Daily Base Wage}}{365} \times \text{Worked Days}, \frac{\text{Eidi Ceiling}}{365} \times \text{Worked Days}\right)$$
- **Tax Treatment:** Eidi is tax-exempt up to the equivalent of one month's basic tax exemption (Annual Art. 84 exemption $\div 12$). Any excess is subject to progressive salary tax withholding. It is 100% exempt from Social Security contributions.
